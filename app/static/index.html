How far can you trust it?

Row-by-row tests on this dataset score close to 100%, but that mostly shows the model recognising species it has already seen. The fairer test is a species it has never seen, and the plain model does much worse there. So it may only answer "edible" when the mushroom closely matches its training data, and any warning odor means "Likely poisonous".

Odor alone turned out to beat all of these models on unseen species (98.0% accuracy). It is too risky to trust for an edible answer, so here a warning odor can only ever lead to "Likely poisonous". An edible answer still needs a close match.

Data: UCI Mushroom dataset, based on The Audubon Society Field Guide to North American Mushrooms (1981).
